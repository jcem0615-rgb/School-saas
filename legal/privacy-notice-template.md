## What we keep, and why

| What | Why we keep it |
|---|---|
| Name, student number, date of birth, grade level, section, programme, photograph | To enrol you, keep a class roll, and issue your ID |
| Guardian names, relationships, phone numbers and email addresses | To reach somebody when you are unwell, absent, or have raised an emergency alert |
| Attendance records, including the location of a scan where your device provides one | To keep the attendance record we are required to keep, and to let your family see that you arrived |
| Marks, submitted work and computed averages | To report your progress and to issue your transcript and Form 137 |
| Fees assessed, payments, receipts, refunds and balances | To bill accurately and give your family a statement they can check |
| Emergency alerts, including device location where available | So your class adviser and the office can find and help you |
| Guidance records and summonses | To support you and to record what the school did about a concern |
| Your email address, role, notification device token, and a record of actions taken in the system | To sign you in, notify you, and trace a change to the person who made it |

## Who sees it

Access depends on the person's role.

- **The office** (Registrar, Admin, Director, Principal) sees enrolment,
  attendance, academic and financial records.
- **Teachers** see the students in the divisions they are assigned to,
  and the marks they themselves record.
- **The guidance office and the Director** see guidance records. Teachers
  and other office staff do not, and they do not appear in the student
  portal.
- **You** see your own records. **A parent** sees the records of a child
  linked to their account.

## Why we are allowed to keep it

We keep most of this because we need it to run the school, to give you
the education you enrolled for, and to meet obligations placed on schools
by `[DepEd / CHED / other regulators as applicable]`. Where we rely on
your consent instead — `[for example, publishing a photograph in school
publicity]` — we will ask for it separately and you can withdraw it.

## What you can ask us for

- **A copy** of what we hold about you.
- **A correction**, if something is wrong.
- **Erasure** — though we cannot always agree. Records we are required to
  keep, and financial records already reported, cannot simply be removed.
  If we say no, we will tell you which records and why.
- **To object** to a particular use.
- **To complain**, to our Data Protection Officer above. If you are not
  satisfied, you can bring your complaint to the National Privacy
  Commission.

You can raise any of these at the school office, or from the Privacy
section of your own profile in the app.

We will respond within `[15]` days.

## If something goes wrong

If personal information is lost or exposed in a way that puts you at
risk, we will tell you and the National Privacy Commission as required.
